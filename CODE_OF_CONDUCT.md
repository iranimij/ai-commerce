# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, criticise ideas and not people, and make room for
people who are new. Harassment and personal attacks are not accepted here.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour in this project makes you uncomfortable, report it privately to the
maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/iranimij). Reports are kept
confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer follows the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant: a private
correction first, then a warning, then a temporary ban, then a permanent ban.
